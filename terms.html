Prototype terms

Terms for using this demo

These terms describe the current public front-end prototype. DeAddict is not yet a production service, medical product, treatment provider, emergency service, or account-based application.

Use the prototype for general educational and design evaluation only. Do not rely on it for diagnosis, treatment, detoxification, emergency decisions, or professional advice.

1. What the prototype provides

The website contains educational material, sample dashboard information, and interactive onboarding and check-in demonstrations. Demonstration answers are not intended to be saved, uploaded, reviewed, or used to make medical decisions.

2. No professional relationship

Using the prototype does not create a doctor-patient, therapist-client, counsellor-client, rehabilitation-provider, legal, fiduciary, or emergency-support relationship. Educational content cannot account for your health history or circumstances.

3. Emergencies and withdrawal risk

Do not use DeAddict to manage an emergency. Contact local emergency services when immediate safety or serious symptoms are involved. Abruptly stopping some substances, including alcohol after regular heavy use, can be dangerous; qualified medical advice may be needed.

4. Your use of the demo

  • Do not enter another person's private information.
  • Do not attempt to bypass, disrupt, scrape, overload, or misuse the website.
  • Do not represent prototype screens as proof that a production treatment or privacy system exists.
  • Do not use public issue trackers to post personal health or recovery details.

5. Availability and accuracy

The prototype may change, become unavailable, contain errors, or show sample data. Content is reviewed in good faith but is not guaranteed to be complete, current for every region, or suitable for an individual's circumstances.

6. External services

Links to helpline directories, public-health sources, or GitHub lead to services controlled by others. Their terms, privacy practices, availability, and content apply when you use them.

7. Intellectual property

The DeAddict name, original interface, text, and visual materials may be protected by applicable intellectual-property laws. Public repository access does not by itself grant permission to use branding or content beyond the repository's applicable licence.

8. Future production terms

Accounts, subscriptions, professional support, data storage, AI features, or regulated functionality would require separate production terms, consent, privacy disclosures, and regional review. None of those systems is active in this prototype.

9. Reporting technical problems

Technical issues may be reported through the public repository issue tracker. Do not include sensitive or identifying information.
