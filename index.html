Core features

Practical tools for everyday moments.

Private daily check-ins

A one-to-two-minute moment to log mood, urges, and what happened — for your eyes only.

Trigger & craving log

See which situations, times, and feelings tend to come before an urge.

Personalized recovery plan

A plan that adjusts as you learn more about what works for you.

Guided grounding exercises

Short breathing and grounding exercises for moments an urge feels strong.

Journaling

A private space to reflect in your own words, whenever you need it.

Milestones

Meaningful markers of progress, without pressure to keep an unbroken streak.

A difficult day does not erase your progress.

Setbacks happen. When they do, DeAddict helps you reflect on what happened, offers a stabilizing next step, and helps you adjust your plan — never a punishment, a shaming screen, or a lost streak.
