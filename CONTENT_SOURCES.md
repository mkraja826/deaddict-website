Last reviewed: July 28, 2026

## Product-content rule

Educational pages must:

- Avoid diagnosing users.
- Avoid instructing potentially dependent users to stop abruptly without medical advice.
- Direct severe withdrawal symptoms and medical emergencies to emergency care.
- Distinguish the DeAddict wellness prototype from treatment, therapy, rehabilitation, and crisis services.
- Be reviewed again before production launch and localized for each supported region.
