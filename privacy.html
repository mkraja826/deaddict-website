Current prototype notice

Privacy at this stage

The current DeAddict website is a front-end prototype. It does not provide accounts and is not intended to save or upload onboarding, check-in, journal, goal, trigger, mood, or recovery answers.

Important: Do not enter identifying, medical, financial, or emergency information into this prototype. Production privacy controls have not been implemented.

Information entered into demos

Onboarding and check-in choices are used only to control what appears on the current page. The Phase 2 design requires those choices to remain in JavaScript memory and disappear when the page is refreshed or closed. They must not be written to local storage, session storage, cookies, IndexedDB, URLs, analytics, or a backend.

Small device preference

The sample dashboard may store a light-or-dark theme preference in your browser. This preference does not contain a recovery answer, account identifier, or health record. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data.

Hosting and technical logs

When the site is deployed, the hosting or network provider may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamps, and security logs. The final hosting provider, retention details, and production privacy contact have not yet been selected.

External support links

Some urgent-support links open an external helpline directory. When you follow an external link, that service receives the request and applies its own privacy practices. DeAddict does not control that external service.

What is not active

  • No authentication or social login
  • No cloud database or file storage
  • No analytics, advertising pixels, or session replay
  • No billing, subscriptions, or payment processing
  • No email collection, newsletters, or marketing automation
  • No production export, deletion, consent, or account-recovery controls

Before sensitive storage is enabled

A future product must add explicit consent, data minimization, encryption, user isolation, export, permanent deletion, session revocation, incident response, retention rules, threat modelling, and regional legal review. Public copy will be updated only after those controls are implemented and tested.

Questions or accessibility reports

Until a dedicated support address exists, technical issues may be reported through the public GitHub issue tracker. Do not post personal health, recovery, contact, or identifying information there.
